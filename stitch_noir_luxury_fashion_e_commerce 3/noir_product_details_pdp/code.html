Manifesto of Form

The Architecture of Evening Tailoring

Inspired by the dramatic sartorial lines of 1970s Le Smoking and contemporary monolith architecture, the Structured Silk Lapel Blazer rejects unnecessary embellishment. The eye travels along the pure diagonal plane of the Como silk lapel, punctuated only by hand-sewn buttonholes and a razor-sharp chest welt.
