Origin Verified

Biella Mills, Piedmont (Italy) • Atelier Rue Saint-Honoré, Paris

Savoir-Faire

Raw Integrity.
Artisanal Mastery.

Every piece begins its journey in historic textile mills in Biella, Italy, where heritage looms spin virgin merino fibers into dense, sculptural cloths with zero microplastic composites.

The garments are assembled by hand in our Paris atelier. Each seam is hand-pressed, canvas interlinings are individually shaped to mirror the human form, and hand-stitched horn buttons seal an enduring bond between artisan and patron.
